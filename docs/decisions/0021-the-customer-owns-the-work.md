# 0021. The customer owns the code, drawings, domain and access

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-20
- **Deciders:** client (Codraft team), Claude

## Context

Who owns a customer's domain and code was an open business question (gap G2 in [content.md](../content.md#4-content-gaps), `PROGRESS.md` item E), meant for Home's "Kako radimo" and the services page. In the review of Home (batch 10), the client wrote the three commitments of "Kako radimo" themselves. The third is: "Sve ostaje vaše: Kod, crteži, domena i pristupi pripadaju vama, i ako jednog dana odete drugdje."

## Decision

**The customer owns what Codraft makes and runs for them:** the code, the drawings, the domain and the access (accounts, passwords, hosting). It stays the customer's if they move to another provider. The site says so in "Kako radimo" on Home, in the client's words.

## Consequences

- G2 is answered. The services page and the FAQ (G8, "Tko je vlasnik…") follow it in phase 3.
- Contracts and offers must match the promise: domains registered in the customer's name, access handed over, the code and drawings delivered with their source files. Third-party licences (fonts, plugins, software) stay under their own terms, and the FAQ can say so.
- The client proofreads every text before the public launch (review batch 10), this one included.
