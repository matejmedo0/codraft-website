# 0034. The new Home's wordmark in the Midnight palette

- **Status:** Accepted; supersedes the wordmark's colour in [0033](0033-new-home-by-the-clients-rules.md)
- **Date:** 2026-10-23
- **Deciders:** client (Codraft team), Claude

## Context

ADR 0033 put the new Home on the Midnight palette and kept the wordmark's brand blue `#2F6BFF`, as in every palette explored at `/lab/paleta`. On the new page that blue measures 4.0:1 on the background, enough for a graphic, but it is the only saturated blue on a page of muted teals. The client asked for the logo's colour to have good contrast and to fit the palette (M14 in `docs/phases/3-pages.md`).

## Decision

On the new Home the wordmark takes the palette: its lettering in Soft ivory `#ECEDEA` (15.4:1 on `#0B181D`) and its three accent details (the arc on the "c", the drafting line, the CAD grip) in Muted teal `#82BBC5` (8.5:1), the page's accent. The shapes are unchanged; only the colours follow the page.

## Consequences

- The wordmark on the new page no longer shows the brand blue. The brand package's masters and the current Home are unchanged.
- If the new Home goes live, the brand notes (`docs/brand.md`), the favicon and the social images need a decision of their own, so the marks don't differ between the page and the browser tab.
