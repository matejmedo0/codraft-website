## Why codraft.hr

- It is the strongest signal of a Croatian business, to Google and to local buyers.
- It matches the brand exactly.
- One domain serves both languages, so there is no duplicate-content or hreflang complexity across domains.
- The name is contested elsewhere (.com, .dev, .eu, .studio, .io are taken), so securing .hr now matters.

## .hr registration rules

Source: Pravilnik NN 83/2023. Confidence: medium-high; confirm with the registrar.

- A legal person or craft registered in Croatia gets **one free .hr domain**, provided the domain contains the registered name or an abbreviation of it (Arts. 12(1), 15(2), 16).
- Other .hr domains are paid and go through authorised registrars. They are open to EU legal persons and to EU nationals with a Croatian OIB (Arts. 11(2), 12(2)).
- **In practice:** if the registered company name contains "Codraft", codraft.hr can be the free CARNET domain. If not, a founder can register it today as a paid domain.

## Name risk

Another "Codraft" (codraft.dev) operates in software and AI, close to Codraft's own offer.

- **Before print or ads,** search "CODRAFT" in TMview (EUIPO) and in the Croatian IP office (DZIV) databases, in Nice classes 9 (software), 35 (business services) and 42 (IT services).
- **For SEO,** brand searches for "Codraft" will also show codraft.dev. Mitigations:
  - local signals: "Codraft" + city, Google Business Profile (if eligible), consistent business data
  - `sameAs` links in the Organization structured data
