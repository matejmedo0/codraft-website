# Pricing research (2026-10-22)

> **Phase 3 input for the Usluge page (G7), not a decision.** On 2026-10-22 the client asked (M6, [3-pages.md](../phases/3-pages.md)): "Za cijene usluga istraži aktualne tržišne cijene usporedivih usluga i postavi okvirno 10 % niže cijene. Dokumentiraj izvore i pretpostavke." This note holds the sources, the arithmetic and the assumptions behind the guide prices in `src/config/pricing.ts`. The prices themselves are a decision and get an ADR.
>
> **How it was collected.** Four research agents, one per group (websites and stores; apps, integrations and AI; Run & Grow; drafting), read providers' own price pages, price guides and marketplaces. A separate fact-checker per group re-opened the cited URLs the same day and confirmed, corrected or rejected each row. Only confirmed rows feed the medians. Rows that failed the check are listed in [section 7](#7-dropped-and-unconfirmed-rows) and are not used.
>
> **Conventions**
> - Accessed on 2026-10-22. Prices in euros.
> - **VAT:** *excl.* means the page says the price is without PDV. *incl.* means it includes 25 % PDV. *none* means the provider is outside the VAT system, so the price is final. *not stated* means the page doesn't say, which is the case on most Croatian pages.
> - **Type:** one-off, monthly, yearly or hourly. "Not stated" means the page doesn't say whether a build price is one-off (the fact-checkers' reading).
> - **Confidence:** the researcher's rating, then the fact-checker's result. High means the provider's own page with clear terms. Medium means a range, an editorial guide, an undated page or ambiguous terms. Low means stale, self-contradictory or a single logged job.
> - **Median:** "yes" means the row feeds the median, with the figure used. "sens." means it is used only in a sensitivity check. "—" means it is context only.
> - Foreign prices use the researchers' rates (1 USD ≈ €0.86, 1 CHF ≈ €1.07, 1 GBP ≈ €1.16, €1 ≈ 4.25 PLN, €1 ≈ 117 RSD), which were not checked against the ECB. The [hosting note](2026-10-19-hosting.md) used $1 ≈ €0.89. Foreign prices are anchors only, so the difference changes no proposal.

## Method

1. **Scope.** Each starting price covers the smallest job that still delivers what the service promises in [content.md](../content.md) ("Što dobivate"). The scope is written under each proposal.
2. **Comparable set.** Croatian providers that publish a price for that scope.
   - For builds, the researcher's "tier (a)" is listed but left out of the median. These are budget template sellers: DTweb, Voxern, Insertio, Pixelit, ZiCoding and webstranica.hr. Codraft designs and codes each site and doesn't sell templates.
   - Editorial guides, regional prices and EU prices are cross-checks, not inputs.
3. **One figure per provider:**
   - the package closest to the scope
   - the lower bound of a range, because Codraft's figure is a from-price too
   - the net price where the page states VAT
4. **The median minus 10 %, then rounded:**
   - one-off prices of €100 or more: to the nearest figure ending in 90 (€190, €890, €1,390)
   - amounts under €100: to the nearest €5
   - monthly fees of €100 or more: to the nearest €10

   Rounding leaves each price between about 5 % and 16 % below its median. Each proposal shows its own figure.
5. **VAT is mixed.** Most Croatian pages don't state VAT, so each median mixes net, final (VAT-free) and unstated prices. See [section 6](#6-assumptions).

### 1.7 Proposals

**Presentation site: od 890 € (from €890), 3–4 weeks.**
- **Market.** The tier (b) median is €980, and 10 % below is €882, which rounds to €890 (9 % below). With all studios the median is €876, which gives €790.
- **Starting scope:**
  - up to 6 pages, plus the privacy and cookie pages, in one language
  - Codraft's own design, not a template
  - a contact form
  - basic SEO from day one: titles, descriptions, structured data and a sitemap
  - speed measured on mobile and desktop before launch
  - two rounds of changes
  - the client supplies the texts and photos

  Six pages is the middle of the comparable packages: h1, Jupiter and Kovar Start go up to 5, Collagen Business 6, Opus 7, and Collagen Premium and Kovar Standard 10.
- **Costs more:**
  - more pages: the market charges €40–70 per extra page (Lenko, Collagen net, Hortus, h1), median €57; 10 % below is about €50
  - a second language
  - a blog or news module
  - copywriting, photography or a logo
  - a booking system or a shop, which are separate lines

### 4.5 Which unit the market uses

- **In Croatia, per plan,** banded by floor area (Tlocrti.com, Public Real Estate), or **by the hour** (€30 for technical work, €30–60 for an architect).
- **Per m²** appears only in an old interior-design list (Sig) and for regulated surveys, which are not comparable.
- **Per sheet** is the norm for paper/PDF-to-DWG work in Serbia (Tekoms) and the US.
- **BIM** is priced per m² or per floor.

Codraft's clients send sketches, scans and markups and get sheets in their own layers and title block. So the proposal is per sheet, with area bands for large plans, as the Croatian per-plan sellers use.

**2D crteži: od 65 € po listu (from €65 per sheet), about 1 week for 1–3 sheets.**

- **Starting scope:**
  - one sheet holding one drawing: a floor plan of up to about 80 m², one section or one elevation

  - DWG and PDF
  - one round of corrections
- **Cross-check.** The Croatian technical hourly rate is about €30 (Geoconsult €30 + PDV; architects €30–60; Zagreb freelancers typically ≈ €21.50). 10 % below is about €27/h, so €65 is about 2.4 hours. For comparison, German drafting offices start at €100, Swiss digitisers at €134–257, and offshore studios charge €34–69.

  - larger plans, in area bands

## 6. Assumptions

- **VAT status is unknown.** The client hasn't said whether Codraft is in the VAT system; this is company data, PROGRESS.md item A.
  - All proposals are net.
  - **If Codraft is in the VAT system,** the site shows each price "+ PDV", and the client pays 25 % more (€890 + PDV = €1,112.50). The comparison then holds against the agencies' net prices.
  - **If Codraft is outside the VAT system,** the prices are final and the page says "PDV se ne obračunava". A client who can't reclaim VAT then pays 25 % less than the same net price from a VAT-registered agency.
  - Either way the page must say whether VAT is included (ZET Art. 6, compliance C2). `PRICES_INCLUDE_VAT` in `src/config/pricing.ts` covers this.
- **The medians mix VAT bases.** Most Croatian pages don't state VAT. Several low prices come from VAT-free sole traders (DTweb, Voxern, K Studio, Insertio, Disketa, Marketingashi), and some of their advantage is the missing PDV.
- **From-prices.** "Od X €" is the smallest job described under each proposal. The written offer fixes the scope, timeline and price, and the price doesn't change unless the scope does (`services.plan.scope.body`).
- **Content.** For builds, the client supplies the texts and photos. Copywriting, photography and a logo are extra.
- **Excluded third-party fees:**
  - payment providers (section 1.5)
  - e-računi and other subscriptions
  - CMP licences, if a client keeps one
  - the domain
  - AI SaaS, if a client prefers one
- **Hosting is not in build prices.** It is the monthly service. Some competitors include year-1 hosting worth about €39–200.
- **Ownership.** The client owns the work (ADR 0021). No price effect is assumed.
- **The tier is a judgment.** Builds compare Codraft with studios, not template sellers. Sensitivity figures: presentation site €790 with all studios; store €2,290 against custom-design shops; SEO €240 against full local packages only.
- **Freshness.** Many pages show "cijena na dan 10.09.2026"; the researcher didn't check why. Re-check the prices once a year and before launch.
